Matcha

Where is the best matcha for Oakland County?

Matcha has its own corner of the Kiki Kafé menu. Kiki makes its matcha lattes with ceremonial-grade green tea, iced or hot. Choose a classic matcha latte, a strawberry matcha, the caramelized apple matcha from the fall menu, or a matcha açaí bowl with fresh fruit and granola.

From Rochester Hills, Troy or Rochester, it is an M-59 run to Partridge Creek, and DoorDash pickup means your matcha is waiting.

Coffee menus in Oakland County use the same handful of words. Here is what each one means at Kiki Kafé, and who it suits.

Espresso drinksA short, strong shot pulled to order, then built into lattes, cappuccinos and macchiatos.You want a classic café drink, hot or iced.

The room

What makes Kiki Kafé a comfortable place to stay?

Kiki Kafé is built for lingering. A living green wall carries the neon line “meet me at kiki,” woven pendant lights hang over banquettes and wood tables, and a tall palm stands by the counter. Outside, the café opens onto the tree-lined, open-air walk at The Mall at Partridge Creek, with patio seating in warm weather.

Guests notice. Google reviewers describe “plenty of comfortable seating,” a “beautiful cozy ambiance” and a café that is “always really clean and cozy.” It suits a catch-up with a friend, a quiet afternoon with a matcha, or dessert after a movie at the mall.
