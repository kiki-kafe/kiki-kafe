Planning

How much does a coffee cart cost in Michigan?

A coffee cart price depends on the hours of service, the guest count and the drink menu, so ask each vendor for a written quote. Kiki Kafé replies to Kiki Kart requests with availability and pricing within 2 to 3 business days.

Five questions to ask any coffee cart vendor
  1. How many baristas come, and how many guests can they serve each hour?
  2. What does the venue need to supply: power, water or floor space?
  3. Which drinks, milks and syrups are on the menu, and are cups and signs included?
  4. Can you send a certificate of liability insurance if the venue asks for one?
  5. What are the price, the hours and the deposit, in writing?

Do I need to provide power for a coffee cart?

What permits does a coffee cart need in Michigan?

Michigan licenses mobile food units through the Michigan Department of Agriculture and Rural Development (MDARD), and local health departments inspect them. Ask any vendor which licence covers their cart.
