Kiki Kafé is a coffee shop inside The Mall at Partridge Creek in Clinton Township, Michigan. The menu has signature lattes from $7.50, ceremonial-grade matcha from $7, organic refreshers at $6, sandwiches at $15, açaí bowls and cheesecakes. Every drink can be made at your sweetness level, with dairy or plant-based milk.
