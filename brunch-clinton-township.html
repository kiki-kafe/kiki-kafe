01

The brunch plates

Kiki's brunch food is three pressed sandwiches, four açaí bowls and a bakery case. The sandwiches are $15 each. The Smoke Bird stacks smoked turkey and pastrami on sourdough with Swiss, greens, pesto aioli, mustard and pickles. The Hot Honey Turkey and The Mozz Garden round out the three.

The açaí bowls run $13, or $15 for the Dubai Pistachio Açaí. The bakery side has six croissants ($6.00 to $7.50), a cheddar jalapeño sourdough bagel ($5.50) and blueberry, pistachio and lemon muffins ($5.00).

03

Brunch with friends, a date or a study group

Kiki's room has banquette seating, wood tables and a green plant wall with a "meet me at kiki" neon sign. Guests write about meeting friends here, dates, and study groups that come every week.

For a big group, come early on a weekend or split into two tables. For a party with a cake, the dessert case has cheesecakes, tiramisu and a No Dye Rainbow Cake ($10.00).

04

Brunch hours

Kiki opens at 10 AM every day. It serves the full menu until 8 PM Monday to Thursday and 9 PM Friday to Sunday, so a late brunch on a Sunday afternoon works too.

Kiki is inside The Mall at Partridge Creek on Hall Road (M-59), with the mall's parking. You can order ahead for pickup online.

05

Gluten-free and allergy notes

The menu names four gluten-free items: the Gluten-Free Chocolate Torte and three gluten-free cookies. The Gluten-Free Vegan Chocolate Chip Cookie is the one item the menu calls vegan. Any drink can be made with an alternative milk for $0.50.

Kiki uses shared equipment and cannot promise any item is free from dairy, eggs, tree nuts, peanuts or wheat. Tell the staff about any allergy.
