How to choose

How do you choose a coffee cart company?

For a wedding or private party, look past the menu to these six points:

  • Presentation. Look at event photos. The cart should suit a formal tent, a garden or a ballroom.
  • Drink range. Espresso, matcha, a seasonal drink and a caffeine-free option cover every age.
  • Timing. Ask how the company handles a 100-guest rush after dinner.
  • Setup footprint. A cart that fits a corner of the room leaves the dance floor alone.
  • Backup plan. Ask what happens if the cart is unavailable on the day.
  • Reply in writing. Availability, hours and price should arrive in one clear message.

Private venue checklist

What should a private venue confirm for a coffee cart?

Estates, tents and clubs set their own rules. Confirm these with the venue or planner:

  • Vendor approval. Some venues keep a list of approved caterers and beverage vendors.
  • Power. An outlet near the cart spot. Kiki’s form asks, and Kiki follows up on a “not sure.”
  • Ground and surface. Lawn, gravel and tent flooring change how a cart sets up. Tell Kiki on the form if the event is indoor, outdoor or both.
  • Arrival path. Gate codes, long driveways and loading rules.
  • Weather. A covered spot for the cart if the forecast turns. Kiki’s Kart has worked under its own tent.

Agreement

What should a wedding coffee cart agreement cover?

Put the following in writing before you pay a deposit:

  • Date, start time and service hours.
  • Deposit, balance due date and refund terms.
  • Menu, including any seasonal drinks.
  • The weather plan for an outdoor event.
