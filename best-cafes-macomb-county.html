How to choose

How do you choose a café in Macomb County?

Five things separate a good café stop from a great one:

  • Hours that fit your day. Many Macomb County cafés close by mid-afternoon. For coffee after work or after dinner, check the closing time first. Kiki Kafé is open until 9 PM Friday to Sunday.
  • A menu beyond coffee. Matcha, refreshers, açaí bowls, sandwiches and desserts turn a coffee run into a meal.
  • Room to stay. Seating, a calm room and a reason to linger.
  • Ordering ahead. Pickup or delivery saves the wait. Kiki Kafé orders through DoorDash.
  • Events. A café that can bring its espresso bar to a wedding, shower or office event.
